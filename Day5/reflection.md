When I ran the same prompt “What is an AI?” multiple times in Google AI Studio with the temperature set to 1, I observed noticeable variations in how the responses were generated. Although all outputs conveyed the core idea of artificial intelligence, they differed in wording, explanation style, and level of detail. One response gave a short and simple definition, while another provided a more detailed explanation with examples. A third response used slightly different terminology and structure, making it more descriptive and informative. These variations show that a higher temperature increases the randomness and creativity of the model, allowing it to produce diverse ways of explaining the same concept rather than giving identical answers each time.
